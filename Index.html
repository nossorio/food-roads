    How to use Food-ROADS

    Every slider starts at status quo. Move a slider to add an intervention; the colored bar on its left marks what you changed. Click ⋯ for what the lever does and which loop it acts on.

    Read the two graphs. Choose any output in each graph's title menu. The thin gray line is the baseline; the bold lines are your scenario. Hover or touch a graph to read values. The shaded band is the intervention period.

    Watch the headline. The yellow number is the low-SES healthy-food share in 2042, the model's equivalent of En-ROADS' temperature: the outcome that tells you whether the market is transforming for everyone.

    Try the scenarios menu for Struben's experiments, then build your own combination. A pathway is convincing when it raises scale, closes the gap, and keeps firms investing after the program ends.

    Assumptions (last column) let you stress-test structure. Conclusions worth presenting should survive reasonable changes.

    Stylized model for strategic discussion, not a forecast. Look and feel inspired by En-ROADS (Climate Interactive and MIT Sloan); this tool is not affiliated with them.
